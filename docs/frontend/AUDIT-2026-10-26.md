## Observations / hypotheses (not yet confirmed defects)

- H1: Search `q` uses ILIKE over `title/summary/full_content` — with 90k rows + trigram GIN this should be OK, but `full_content ILIKE` on big bodies may be slow. Measure in Phase 5.
- H2: `MAX_SUMMARY_LENGTH=200` (words) truncates summaries mid-sentence; acceptable but verify rendering.
- H3: 132 feeds but many near-duplicates (e.g. feedburner wrappers) — affects digest "cross-feed coverage" semantics (same story via wrapper feeds inflates corroboration). Verify with cluster data.
- H4: `WriteTimeout` unset on frontend http.Server (ScoutFrontend note) — slow-client write hang risk. Low.
- H5: 389 failed fetches/24h → stats shows DEGRADED; verify these are per-feed transient (heavy sites), not systemic.
